S3. Human request: "todos los servicios no duran lo mismo, depende que eliga el cliente dura mas o menos". Accepted proposal context: duration is the sum of existing services without headcount scaling; snapshot agreed price and duration; use a configurable 15-minute start grid. Capacity requires simultaneous wash slots and the sum of required staff no greater than the active employee count; future appointments do not assign named employees. Availability and reservation must be transactional.
S4. Human request: "Lo que si tendras que sugerirme vos es el tema de como se gestionaran los insumos". Accepted proposal context: forecast stock at booking; perform the effective existing reservation at wash start, when converting an appointment to a wash. A started appointment closes as completed when its linked wash ends, including when the wash terminates partially; retain the partial details in the wash. This rule does not apply to appointments that have not started and does not define the outcome of a full cancellation.
S5. Human request: "Si un cliente cancela con mas de X tiempo de antelacion configurado en la configuracion del sistema" and "al cliente que cancela se le premia con eso con un descuento". Accepted proposal context: use a single-use coupon with the existing configured expiry. Human choices: the coupon is applied at booking and consumed at confirmation, even if the appointment is later canceled or ends in absence. The coupon takes the place of the wash's "extra" discount and cannot be combined with it; it may optionally be combined with service packages.
