# Shared presentation helpers

Shared helpers retain falsy-safe DOM-text escaping, ordered append of repeated form entries, es-AR/ARS currency formatting and per-consumer sort defaults.
DOM-text escaping differs from the existing quote-escaping regex contract; do not interchange them blindly. Consumers with fallbacks are exercised with and without SiteModule.
The 24-case baseline differential recorded 22 identical cases and two separately authorized vehicle-search repairs; this is bounded fixture evidence.
Focused checks: `node --test Firebase.Tests/SharedPresentationUtilities.test.cjs Firebase.Tests/SharedPresentationConsumers.test.cjs`.
